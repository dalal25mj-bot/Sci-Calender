A message of appreciation💌 A thank-you letter to the scientists

Dear Scientists, Pioneers, Researchers and Curious Minds,

Thank you for looking beyond what was already known. Thank you for asking difficult questions, testing ideas, learning from failure and having the courage to explore the unknown.

Your discoveries have changed how we understand the universe, care for our health, communicate, travel, grow food and protect our planet. Some of you worked in laboratories; others observed the skies, studied living things, developed mathematical ideas, designed instruments or challenged accepted explanations. Every careful measurement, thoughtful question and shared insight has helped move human knowledge forward.

We also remember the teachers, assistants, technicians, collaborators and students whose contributions make scientific progress possible. Knowledge grows when it is shared, questioned and made accessible to everyone.

May science continue to build bridges across cultures, inspire the next generation, support peace and help create a fairer and more sustainable world. Your curiosity has become part of humanity’s shared inheritance.

With gratitude and admiration,
The Science Through the Year community

Keep questioning. Keep discovering. Keep making a difference. ✦

Educator profile👩‍🔬 About Me

Dalal Alotaibi

Science educator · Physics specialist · Curriculum and educational leadership

Science & Physics Educator | Curriculum Alignment | Educational Leadership

I am a physics-qualified educator and curriculum leader with 14 years of experience, including 12 years teaching science and physics. My work is grounded in inquiry, hands-on learning and helping students connect scientific ideas with the world around them.

I have taught the Saudi MOE McGraw-Hill science and physics curriculum and developed working knowledge of the England National Curriculum, NGSS and the IB PYP, MYP and DP. I use approaches such as the 5E instructional model, STEM/STEAM, project-based learning, PhET simulations and augmented reality to make learning active and meaningful.

Alongside classroom teaching, I have coordinated and supported teams of educators, mentored teachers and contributed to quality assurance through KPI and Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning (MEL) practices.

Accuracy & sourcesAbout this collection

Portraits and summaries load from Wikipedia when you are online, and each profile links back to its biography. If a photo cannot load, a coloured monogram appears. Some historical birth dates are disputed or recorded under older calendars, so check the biography before publishing or teaching. Starred scientists are saved on this device only.

Portraits are retrieved from the scientists’ Wikipedia biography pages when available. For people who lived before photography, the image may be a later artistic depiction rather than a photograph. If no verified portrait is available, the card keeps a name monogram instead of showing an unrelated face.
